About MindCheck

A screening and signposting tool

Many people notice changes in their mood, stress or sleep but aren't sure whether it's serious enough to seek help. Stigma, cost and access often delay that step. MindCheck offers three short, widely used questionnaires in private, explains the result in plain language, and points to practical next steps and trusted support.

Safety and privacy

  • If PHQ-9 question 9 (thoughts of self-harm) is answered above 0, a support screen with Tele-MANAS and 112 appears before the result. You can always continue to your result.
  • All scoring and safety checks run on the server, so they can't be skipped from the browser.
  • Without an account, nothing is saved. Results stay in your browser session and disappear when you close it.
  • With an account, only the tool, score, band and date are saved, never your individual answers. You can delete any result or your whole account at any time.
  • Data handling follows the principles of India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023: consent, purpose limitation, minimisation and erasure.
