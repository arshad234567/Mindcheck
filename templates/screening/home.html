How it works

  1. Agree to the short privacy notice.
  2. Answer questions about the last two weeks.
  3. The server scores your answers and checks for safety concerns.
  4. See what your answers suggest, with next steps and support.

Screens and signposts

It does not diagnose, treat, or replace a doctor or counsellor, and never gives medication advice.
